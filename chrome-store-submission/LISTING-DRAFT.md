# Chrome Web Store listing draft

**Name**  
Clonbrow Workspace

**Short description**  
A customizable workspace with theme pages, split panes, and built-in productivity tools, opened from the toolbar when you need it.

**Category**  
Productivity

**Single purpose**  
Clonbrow gives you an optional workspace for opening bundled theme pages and using lightweight productivity tools. Chrome's usual New Tab page stays unchanged; launch Clonbrow from its toolbar button.

**Detailed description**  
Clonbrow opens in its own tab when you choose it from the toolbar. Choose from bundled themes, keep several pages open in split panes, and use built-in writing, formatting, color, calculator, and focus tools.

Your Clonbrow setup, pane session, and pages you create in the workspace are stored in the extension's local browser storage. The extension does not request access to your browsing history or active tabs.

Some bundled themes have optional online features. Depending on the theme and feature used, they may load libraries or images from third-party CDNs, query Wikipedia, or sync account data and items such as tasks, flashcards, quizzes, notes, and formulas with the Clonbrow service at `clonexxshree.pythonanywhere.com`. Review the privacy policy before publishing and ensure these behaviors and destinations are accurate.
